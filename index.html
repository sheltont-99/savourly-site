About Savourly

Savourly is where chefs publish the recipes they actually cook from — one card per dish, tested for a home stove and written the way they'd explain it at their own counter. Browse the kitchens publishing today and buy the cards you want to cook this week.

How it works

One chef, one shopfront, one set of recipe cards.

Each chef on Savourly runs their own page: a short bio, then the recipe cards they've written, priced individually or as a full collection. Cards are printed and posted, so what you buy is something you can prop on the counter while you cook.

Search for a chef by name or cuisine at the top of any page, or open the full directory below.

The short version

Ghanaian technique, wherever you're cooking from.

Amara trained in Michelin kitchens across Europe, then spent two years travelling between family kitchens in Accra, Kumasi and Cape Coast, recording the small adjustments — a second addition of ginger, the exact moment shito turns from red to black — that never make it into a standard recipe.

Every card in her shop is one dish, one method, written in full: no chapter to flip past, no filler. Buy what you want to cook this week.
